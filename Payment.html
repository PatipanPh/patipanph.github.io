Payment

Payment Processing

Flow Overview

A self-service SaaS checkout flow that allows users to select a plan, customize add-ons, review their order, and complete payment seamlessly.

Problem

Users are required to complete too many steps before payment, including mandatory form inputs that increase friction and drop-off rates.

Solution

Simplify the checkout by reducing unnecessary steps and making billing information optional. Handle payment errors inline on the same screen, allowing users to quickly retry without leaving the flow.

Impact

Higher conversion rate due to reduced friction Lower drop-off during payment Improved user experience with a smoother, continuous flow Increased revenue from easier checkout completion
